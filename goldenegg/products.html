Our Poultry

Eggs, meat birds, chicks and layers raised the right way.

Daily supply

Table eggs

Our hens lay every day, and eggs are collected, cleaned, graded and packed on the same day.

  • Sizes: medium, large and extra large
  • Sold in trays of 30 and crates of 360
  • Delivered within 24 hours of laying
Meat birds

Broilers

Fast-growing birds fed on balanced starter, grower and finisher feed for tender meat.

  • Ready for market in 6 to 7 weeks
  • Sold live or dressed and cleaned
  • Monthly batches for shops, schools and hotels
Start-up stock

Day-old chicks

Healthy chicks from vaccinated parent stock, with the first vaccination already given.

  • Broiler and layer chicks available
  • Delivered in ventilated boxes
  • Free guidance on brooding for your first batch
Layers

Point-of-lay pullets

Young hens about 18 weeks old, ready to begin laying. Skip the brooding stage and start earning sooner.

  • Vaccinated and dewormed
  • Raised on layer-grower feed
  • Start laying within a few weeks of delivery

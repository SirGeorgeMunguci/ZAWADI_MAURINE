Who we are

Poultry farming done with care

GoldenEgg started with 100 hens in a backyard coop. Today we supply eggs, meat and chicks to homes, shops, schools and new poultry farmers.

  • Strict biosecurity and a full vaccination programme
  • Quality feed mixed with the right protein and calcium
  • Clean, airy houses with fresh water at all times
  • Free advice for farmers who buy our chicks

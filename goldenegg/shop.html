Questions

Frequently asked questions

How fresh are your eggs?

Eggs are collected daily and delivered within 24 hours of laying. Each tray carries a packing date.

Are your chicks vaccinated?

Yes. Day-old chicks get their first vaccination at the hatchery, and we give you a schedule for the next ones.

Do you deliver outside Mukono?

We deliver daily to Kampala, Mukono, Entebbe and Jinja. Other districts are served twice a week. Chicks travel in ventilated boxes.

Can you help me start a poultry farm?

Yes. When you buy chicks from us, we advise you on housing, brooding, feeding and vaccination for free.

How can I pay?

We accept cash on delivery, MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money.

About GoldenEgg

A family poultry farm that grew from one backyard coop.

Our story

From 100 hens to a trusted farm

In 2014, the Mugisha family built a small coop behind their home in Mukono and bought 100 laying hens. Neighbours loved the fresh eggs, and orders grew week by week.

Today GoldenEgg keeps thousands of birds, supplies shops and schools, and sells healthy chicks to farmers who want to start their own poultry business.

Our mission: to provide safe, affordable eggs and meat, and to help young people build successful poultry farms.

Our journey

Milestones

  1. 2014 – The first 100 hens
    A small backyard coop starts supplying the neighbourhood.
  2. 2017 – First poultry house
    A purpose-built house for 1,500 layers opens.
  3. 2019 – Broilers added
    We begin raising broilers in batches for shops and schools.
  4. 2022 – Chick sales and training
    New farmers can buy vaccinated chicks and learn from our team.
  5. 2025 – 5,000 layers
    Egg production reaches thousands of eggs every day.
What guides us

Our values

🤝 Honesty

Fair prices, accurate egg grading and clear advice for every customer.
